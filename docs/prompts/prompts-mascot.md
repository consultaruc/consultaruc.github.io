# ConsultaRUC.pe — 5 Mascot Logo Prompts (gov-mascot, peruano, con significado)

5 mascotas **distintas y con significado**, con feel de servicio público/gobierno peruano: confiables pero amigables, memorables, "que dan buena onda". Paleta: **navy #16233F**, **naranja #F97316 / #EA5B0C**, blanco, toque de **rojo bandera #DC2626** y verde de estado #16A34A. Estilo: **flat vector mascot** moderno, limpio, tipo govtech friendly (no caricatura infantil, no 3D pesado). Cada prompt es autónomo — cópialo en Nano Banana Pro / Ideogram / Midjourney. Pide siempre: (1) mascota de cuerpo/busto sobre fondo transparente, (2) versión ícono compacto (cabeza/cara) para favicon y avatar, (3) el lockup con el wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe".

> **Regla:** nunca uses el escudo nacional del Perú ni el logo real de la SUNAT. La mascota es un personaje original.

---

## MASCOT 1 — "Cóndor Verificador" (el que todo lo ve) ⭐ recomendado

```
Design a friendly, professional MASCOT logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a free Peruvian portal to look up any company's RUC (tax ID). The mascot is an ANDEAN CONDOR — Peru's national bird — reimagined as a helpful, trustworthy public-service character. Meaning: the condor "sees everything across Peru from above", so it perfectly represents oversight, search and verification. Draw it in a clean modern flat-vector mascot style: a rounded, approachable condor with a confident but kind expression, its characteristic white neck ruff, dark navy (#16233F) body and wings, and a warm-orange (#F97316) beak. Give it two subtle brand cues: it holds a MAGNIFYING GLASS in one wing (the search action), and wears a small orange lanyard/ID badge or a tiny check-mark pin on its chest (the "verified/official" idea). Keep the wings tidy and geometric, not messy feathers, so it scales down. The pose is welcoming — slight lean forward, ready to help, not aggressive. Personality: reliable, civic, a little proud, but friendly — like the mascot of a national digital service. Add a soft rounded navy badge/disc behind it as an optional containing shape for the icon version. Beside the mascot, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe" in a bold geometric sans (Sora spirit), navy with ".pe" in orange. Deliver: (1) mascot bust + full lockup, (2) a compact CONDOR-HEAD icon (just head + magnifier) that works at favicon size, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat vector, thick clean shapes, high recognizability at 24px, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no scary/realistic bird, no national coat of arms, no SUNAT logo. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**Por qué:** ave nacional del Perú + "ve todo / supervisa" = significado perfecto para búsqueda y verificación. Institucional y peruano a la vez.

## MASCOT 2 — "Llamita Oficial" (la más peruana y querible)

```
Design a warm, memorable MASCOT logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a Peruvian RUC lookup service. The mascot is a friendly LLAMA (or vicuña) — instantly Peruvian, approachable and trustworthy. Meaning: the llama is the friendly local guide that helps you find and verify any company's RUC. Style: clean modern flat-vector mascot, rounded and huggable but still professional — cream/off-white fur with a navy (#16233F) accent blanket/poncho on its back carrying a subtle Andean geometric pattern in orange (#F97316), big friendly eyes, a small confident smile. Brand cues: the llama holds or looks through a MAGNIFYING GLASS (search), and wears a tiny official ID lanyard or a little orange cap/badge (the "official service" idea); optionally a small green check floating near it for "verified/active". Keep the silhouette simple and iconic so the head alone is recognizable. Personality: helpful, cheerful, honest — the trustworthy neighbor who knows the paperwork. Optionally place it inside a soft rounded navy disc for the icon version. Beside it, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe" in a bold geometric sans (Sora spirit), navy with an orange ".pe". Deliver: (1) full mascot + lockup, (2) a compact LLAMA-HEAD icon (head + tiny magnifier or badge) for favicon/avatar, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat vector, thick clean shapes, adorable but not childish, legible at 24px, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no SUNAT logo, no national shield, no touristy clichés (no rainbow overload). The Andean pattern on the blanket should be subtle and elegant, not busy. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**Por qué:** camélido = símbolo peruano universal, cercano y fácil de recordar. Máxima simpatía + identidad nacional.

Meaning: a helpful government-desk clerk who instantly finds and verifies the RUC for you. Style: clean modern flat-vector mascot, rounded and simple, professional but warm. Give him a neat navy (#16233F) uniform or blazer with a small orange (#F97316) tie or pocket accent, a friendly face with a confident smile, and a little official cap or an ID badge/lanyard reading a tiny "RUC" tag. Brand cues: he holds a MAGNIFYING GLASS over a small document card (the ficha), and a subtle green check floats to signal "verified/active". Keep him inclusive and neutral (approachable Peruvian public-servant vibe), not a caricature. The pose: welcoming, one hand presenting the magnifier, "here, let me help you". Optionally frame the icon version in a soft rounded navy disc. Beside the mascot, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe" in a bold geometric sans (Sora spirit), navy with an orange ".pe", optional micro-tag "Consulta y verifica". Deliver: (1) full mascot + lockup, (2) a compact HEAD/BUST icon (face + cap + magnifier) for favicon/avatar, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat vector, thick clean shapes, friendly and trustworthy, legible at 24px, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no real SUNAT logo/uniform, no national coat of arms. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**Por qué:** el "funcionario amable" comunica exactamente el servicio (consultar/verificar) de forma humana y confiable.

Meaning: the record you're looking for, personified — approachable and trustworthy. Style: clean flat-vector mascot. Draw a rounded white document card with a bold navy (#16233F) outline and a warm-orange (#F97316) left spine (the brand's cadastral signature); on its "face" put two friendly dot-eyes and a small smile, and a few simplified content lines plus a tiny SEGMENTED RUC hint (three little chips, last one orange). Give it small simple arms — one waving hello, one holding a MAGNIFYING GLASS — and a tiny green check badge in a corner for "verified/active". Keep it clean and iconic so the card-face reads at favicon size. Personality: helpful, honest, cheerful — "hi, I'm your record, let me help you check it". Optionally set it inside a soft rounded navy disc for the icon version. Beside it, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe" in a bold geometric sans (Sora spirit), navy with an orange ".pe". Deliver: (1) full Fichita + lockup, (2) a compact CARD-FACE icon for favicon/avatar, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat vector, thick clean shapes, friendly but professional, legible at 24px, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no paper texture, no SUNAT logo, no national coat of arms. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**Por qué:** conecta directísimo con el producto (la ficha RUC) y le da carácter; original y fácil de recordar.
