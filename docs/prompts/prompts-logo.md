# ConsultaRUC.pe — 5 Logo Prompts (institucional, memorable, es-PE)

5 conceptos **distintos entre sí**, todos con feel de organización/institución peruana, simples y fáciles de recordar. Paleta de marca: **navy #16233F**, **naranja #F97316** (acento) y **#EA5B0C** (sombra), blanco #FFFFFF, y toques opcionales del rojo bandera #DC2626. Tipografía en el espíritu de **Sora** (geométrica, fuerte). Cada prompt es autónomo: cópialo tal cual en el generador (Nano Banana Pro / Midjourney / Ideogram). Genera en vector limpio, fondo transparente o blanco, y en versión horizontal (lockup) + versión ícono (isotipo) suelto.

---

## LOGO 1 — Monograma "CR" en tile redondeado (fintech-govtech)

```
Design a modern, institutional logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a free public portal to look up Peruvian tax IDs (RUC). Core concept: a bold, friendly LETTERMARK monogram "CR" set inside a rounded square tile (12–16% corner radius), the same shape language as a trustworthy fintech app icon. The tile uses a subtle top-to-bottom gradient from #223a63 to deep navy #16233F, with the "CR" letters in clean white, drawn in a geometric bold sans-serif in the spirit of Sora (weight 800), tightly kerned so the C and R feel like one confident unit. Add ONE small brand signal: a short warm-orange (#F97316) accent bar or underline anchored to the base of the letters — a single deliberate stroke, never decorative clutter. To the right of the tile, place the wordmark on two lines: "Consulta" in medium navy and "RUC Perú" in bold navy, aligned to the tile's optical center, with generous letter spacing for an official, calm, organized feel. The overall mood: authoritative but approachable, like a modern Peruvian government-tech service or a national registry brand — think the trust of an institution with the polish of Stripe or Linear. Keep it extremely simple and instantly memorable: a person should be able to redraw the "CR tile" from memory. Deliver a coordinated set: (1) full horizontal lockup, (2) the standalone "CR" tile as an app icon / favicon, (3) a single-color navy version and a reversed white-on-navy version for dark backgrounds. Requirements: flat vector, crisp edges, perfect geometric alignment, no photorealism, no gradients that look cheap, no drop shadows, no gloss of legibility at 24px. Correct Spanish spelling. Do NOT use the real SUNAT emblem, the Peruvian coat of arms, or any official government seal. Background: transparent (and one variation on solid white).
```

**ALT / uso:** ícono "CR" para favicon, app y avatar; lockup horizontal para el header.

## LOGO 2 — Perú + lupa (búsqueda del país)

```
Design a clean, memorable logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a Peruvian RUC lookup service. Core concept: fuse the SILHOUETTE OF PERU with a MAGNIFYING GLASS to say "search across Peru" in one glance. Render the country as a simplified, smooth single-color navy (#16233F) shape — recognizable but stylized, not a detailed map — and let a magnifying glass integrate naturally: its circular lens overlaps the coast where Lima sits, and a small warm-orange dot (#F97316) marks Lima precisely inside or at the edge of the lens. The magnifier handle is a short, confident stroke in orange #EA5B0C angling down-right. Keep negative space smart: the lens can "reveal" a tiny segmented RUC hint (three tiny bars) or simply frame the Lima dot — pick the cleaner option. To the right, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC" in bold geometric navy sans (Sora spirit) with ".pe" as a small orange tag, and a thin tagline slot underneath for "RUC · SUNAT · Perú" in muted slate (optional, removable). The mood is that of a national public utility: trustworthy, civic, calm, and unmistakably Peruvian, but modern and light — not touristy, not clip-art. It must read instantly even at favicon size, where only the Peru+lens mark shows. Deliver: (1) horizontal lockup, (2) standalone Peru-magnifier isotipo, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat minimal vector, one or two colors max in the icon, thick enough strokes to survive small sizes, perfect balance, no photorealism, no cluttered map details, no generic stock magnifier. Correct Spanish. Do NOT use the official Peru coat of arms, the SUNAT logo, or any government seal — only a neutral, stylized country silhouette. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**ALT / uso:** transmite "Perú + búsqueda"; ideal para redes y OG image.

## LOGO 3 — Sello de verificación (confianza institucional)

```
Design an institutional, trust-first logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a portal that lets anyone verify a Peruvian company's RUC (tax status). Core concept: a modern VERIFICATION SEAL — a rounded badge/medallion that signals "checked, official, reliable" without copying any real government emblem. Build a soft-edged shield-meets-seal shape in deep navy #16233F with a subtle 1px inner ring; at its center place a bold check mark in warm orange #F97316, clean and geometric, as the hero element. Around the check, add a restrained ring of institutional detail: a thin dotted or notched circular border (like a stamp) and, at the top arc, three tiny segmented blocks hinting at the RUC structure (2–8–1). Optionally, a very subtle Peru silhouette or a small red-white flag chip tucked at the seal's base for national identity — keep it whisper-quiet, never busy. To the right, set "ConsultaRUC.pe" in a strong navy geometric sans (Sora spirit, weight 700), with a small "VERIFICADO" or "RUC · SUNAT" micro-label in orange caps beneath, evoking an official reference service. The mood: credible, secure, governmental-but-friendly — the badge you'd trust before doing business, like the seal of a national registry or a certification authority. It must remain crisp and legible as a small circular icon (the seal alone works as favicon/avatar). Deliver: (1) horizontal lockup, (2) standalone seal isotipo, (3) single-color navy and reversed white-on-navy variants. Requirements: flat vector, balanced radial symmetry, confident thick check, no photorealistic metal/emboss, no cheap gradients, legible at 24px. Correct Spanish. Do NOT reproduce the SUNAT logo, the Peruvian national shield/coat of arms, or any real official seal — create an original, neutral verification mark. Background transparent, plus a white-background version.
```

**ALT / uso:** refuerza confianza; combina con el "Sello Verificado" embebible del sitio.

## LOGO 4 — Ficha/documento con dígitos RUC (registro)

```
Design a distinctive logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe" built around the idea of the RUC RECORD (la ficha): a stylized DOCUMENT CARD as the icon. Draw a clean rounded rectangle "card" standing slightly upright, in white with a crisp navy (#16233F) outline and a bold warm-orange (#F97316) vertical spine on its left edge — the brand's cadastral-document signature. On the card, suggest a RUC ficha with two or three simplified content lines and, as the hero detail, a small SEGMENTED RUC BLOCK: three tiny rounded chips reading in monospace "20 · ● · ●" or three abstract bars grouped 2–8–1, with the last chip highlighted orange. Add a tiny green check in the top-right corner of the card to signal "verified / active". The card should feel like an official record you can pull instantly — organized, precise, trustworthy. Keep it geometric and minimal so the card icon alone is memorable and works at favicon size. To the right, place the wordmark "ConsultaRUC" in bold navy geometric sans (Sora spirit) with ".pe" as an orange tag, and an optional micro-tagline "Ficha RUC · SUNAT" in muted slate. The mood: a modern national registry / official reference tool — the digital equivalent of pulling a clean printed record, but friendly and light, in the visual family of govtech and fintech products. Deliver: (1) horizontal lockup, (2) standalone document-card isotipo, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat vector, sharp corners and consistent stroke weights, strong figure/ground, no photorealism, no paper texture, no skeuomorphic shadows, legible at 24px, correct Spanish, monospace look only for the RUC digits. Do NOT use the real SUNAT logo, the Peru coat of arms, or any government seal. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**ALT / uso:** conecta directo con "Ficha RUC"; refuerza el eje documento/registro.

## LOGO 5 — Isotipo "escudo-hexágono" con chispa naranja (marca ágil)

```
Design a bold, ultra-memorable emblem logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a fast, free Peruvian RUC lookup service. Core concept: a single strong ISOTYPE — a rounded HEXAGON (or soft shield-hex) in deep navy #16233F that reads as a stable, institutional "node/registry", crossed by a decisive warm-orange (#F97316) element that conveys SPEED and SEARCH: either a stylized magnifier/lightning hybrid or an orange chevron/spark cutting through the hexagon's negative space. Inside the hexagon, carve a clean geometric mark from negative space — ideally an abstract "R" or a magnifier lens — so the icon feels like a considered monogram, not a generic badge. The hexagon suggests structure, network and reliability (a data node / official registry); the orange spark suggests instant results. Keep exactly two colors in the icon (navy + orange) plus optional white cut-outs, for maximum recall and easy printing. To the right, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe" in a confident navy geometric sans in the spirit of Sora (weight 700–800), tightly spaced, with ".pe" subtly in orange. The mood: modern, agile and dependable — a national digital service that is quick yet official, like the brand of a govtech startup backed by an institution. It must be instantly recognizable and redrawable from memory, and must survive as a tiny favicon (the hexagon-with-spark alone). Deliver: (1) horizontal lockup, (2) standalone hexagon isotipo, (3) single-color navy and reversed white-on-navy variants. Requirements: flat vector, precise geometry, balanced hexagon, clean negative-space cut, no photorealism, no gradients or bevels, legible at 24px, correct Spanish. Do NOT use the SUNAT logo, the Peruvian coat of arms, or any official government seal. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**ALT / uso:** el más "marca/startup"; fuerte como app icon y sello social.
