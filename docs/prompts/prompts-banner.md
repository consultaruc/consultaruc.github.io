# ConsultaRUC.pe — Banner / cover para redes sociales

Banner para portada de redes (Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn). Genera dos tamaños del mismo diseño:
- **1500 × 500** (cabecera de X/Twitter y uso general)
- **1200 × 628** (portada/enlace, reutilizable para OG)

Paleta: navy #16233F → #20355c (fondo), naranja #F97316 (acento), dorado #E0B057 (sello), blanco. Estilo institucional/govtech, limpio, confiable. Incluye el sello dorado de la marca.

```
Design a professional social-media BANNER (cover image) for "Consulta RUC Perú", a free Peruvian public portal to look up any company's RUC (tax ID). Institutional govtech look — trustworthy, clean, modern, NOT flashy. Canvas: wide banner, 1500x500 (and a 1200x628 variant with the same layout re-balanced). Background: a deep-navy gradient from #20355c (top-left) to #14203a (bottom-right), with a very faint single-line silhouette of the map of Peru on the right side (6-8% opacity) and a tiny warm-orange dot marking Lima. Add a thin warm-orange (#F97316) accent bar along the full left edge.

LEFT/CENTER content: a large, confident white headline "Consulta RUC Perú" in a bold geometric sans (Sora spirit, weight 800, tight spacing). Below it, a one-line subheading in light slate (#c7d2e3): "Verifica cualquier empresa o persona — estado, razón social y ficha, con datos de la SUNAT." Under that, two small pills: a solid orange pill reading "100% GRATIS" (white bold text) and an outlined/translucent pill reading "18.3M registros · sin registro". At the very bottom-left, the domain in monospace warm-gold (#E0B057): "consultaruc-pe.com".

RIGHT content: place the brand's circular GOLD SEAL badge (an official-style stamp: gold ring, navy center, a small magnifier + check monogram, "CONSULTA RUC · REGISTRO PÚBLICO · PERÚ" curved text, a tiny Peru flag) as the hero graphic, large and crisp, floating with a soft shadow. Optionally add 3-4 subtle floating chips of the data fields it returns (RUC, ESTADO, HABIDO, DOMICILIO) as faint navy cards behind/around the seal, to hint at the product — keep them very subtle so the composition stays clean.

Overall: balanced, breathable, official and reassuring — like the cover of a national digital public service. Flat vector, crisp edges, high contrast for legibility at small sizes, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no cheap gradients, no clutter. Do NOT use the real SUNAT logo, the Peruvian national coat of arms, gob.pe or Marca Perú — the seal is an original mark. Deliver the 1500x500 and a 1200x628 version, plus one plain-navy version without the map watermark. Leave safe margins so nothing important is cropped by profile-photo overlays on the left.
```

**Tip de recorte:** en Facebook/LinkedIn la foto de perfil tapa la esquina inferior-izquierda; por eso el dominio va abajo-izquierda pero con margen, y el sello a la derecha. Pide siempre una versión con “safe area” respetada.
