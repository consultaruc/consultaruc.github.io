# ConsultaRUC.pe — 5 Logos estilo INSTITUCIONAL / gobierno (es-PE)

Look serio y oficial, como el de un **portal del Estado peruano / registro / superintendencia** (gob.pe, entidades públicas): autoritario, confiable, limpio, simbólico — **nada de caricaturas ni mascotas**. Paleta: **navy #16233F** (institucional), **naranja #F97316 / #EA5B0C** (acento de marca), blanco, y toque de **rojo bandera #DC2626** cuando aporte identidad peruana; opcional dorado/ámbar #D97706 para el sol andino. Tipografía institucional geométrica (espíritu Sora / Inter), en MAYÚSCULAS para tags oficiales. Cada prompt es autónomo. Pide siempre: (1) marca principal, (2) versión ícono/emblema suelto para favicon, (3) versión mono navy y reversa blanca.

> **Regla:** NO reproducir el escudo nacional del Perú, el logo real de la SUNAT, la marca "gob.pe" ni la "Marca Perú". Crear un emblema **original** con estética institucional.

---

## LOGO 1 — Emblema/Sello oficial circular ⭐ (el más "gobierno")

```
Design an official, institutional EMBLEM logo for "ConsultaRUC.pe", a public-interest Peruvian portal to consult company tax IDs (RUC). It must look like the seal of a national registry or a state superintendencia — authoritative, trustworthy, timeless — NOT a cartoon or mascot. Build a clean circular SEAL: a deep navy (#16233F) ring with fine institutional detail (a thin double outline and small evenly-spaced notches or dots, like an official stamp). Around the top arc, set curved uppercase text "CONSULTA RUC" and around the bottom arc "PERÚ · SUNAT PÚBLICO" in a geometric institutional sans, small and precise. In the center, place ONE strong original symbol on a light field: a stylized DOCUMENT/registry card fused with a MAGNIFYING GLASS (search + record), rendered in navy with a warm-orange (#F97316) accent (the lens rim or a check). Optionally add a subtle horizontal red-white-red flag bar (#DC2626 + white) beneath the central symbol as a discreet Peruvian cue. The whole emblem should feel engraved and official, balanced and radially symmetric, but modern and flat (no metallic 3D, no heavy shading). Beside or below the seal, set the wordmark "ConsultaRUC.pe" in bold institutional navy sans, with ".pe" in orange. Deliver: (1) full emblem + wordmark lockup, (2) the circular seal alone as a favicon/avatar (must read at 24px — simplify the inner symbol for the tiny version), (3) single-color navy and reversed white-on-navy variants. Requirements: flat vector, crisp geometry, perfectly centered, legible micro-text at medium size, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no gradients that look cheap. Do NOT use the real Peruvian coat of arms, the SUNAT logo, the gob.pe mark or Marca Perú. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.
```

**Por qué:** el sello circular es EL lenguaje visual de las entidades públicas. Máxima sensación de oficialidad y confianza.

## LOGO 2 — Marca digital estilo gob.pe (institucional moderna, minimal)

To its left, place ONE simple, meaningful ICON in a rounded-square navy tile: a minimal MAGNIFYING GLASS whose lens frames a tiny document/registry line or a check — communicating "search + verify official record". Keep the icon ultra-clean with 2px geometric strokes, navy with a single orange accent. Under the wordmark, allow a thin institutional tagline slot in muted slate uppercase: "PORTAL DE CONSULTA · DATOS PÚBLICOS SUNAT". Add a discreet Peruvian cue: a tiny vertical red-white-red flag chip (#DC2626 + white) at the start of the tagline. The overall feel: like the header brand of a national digital government platform — modern, orderly, credible, quietly authoritative. It must scale down: the tile-icon alone works as favicon. Deliver: (1) full horizontal lockup, (2) the icon tile alone for favicon/app, (3) monochrome navy and reversed white versions. Requirements: flat vector, precise alignment to a baseline grid, legible at 24px, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no gradients, no clutter. Do NOT use the real SUNAT logo, the gob.pe mark, Marca Perú or the national coat of arms. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.

**Por qué:** limpio y moderno como los servicios digitales del Estado. Muy legible y versátil.

Requirements: flat vector, precise radial geometry, legible at 24px, correct Spanish, no photorealism, no cheap gradients. Do NOT use the real SUNAT logo, the national coat of arms, gob.pe or Marca Perú. Background transparent, plus one white-background version.

**Por qué:** la estampa/roundel se lee al instante como "sello oficial" — confianza institucional pura.
